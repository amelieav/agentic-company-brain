# Key Suppliers

- **Spirulina extract:** AlgaeWorks Ltd (Cornwall). Lead time 9 days, minimum order 25kg. Sole approved source; backup supplier BlueBloom GmbH is approved but 18% pricier and has a slightly greener hue, so needs a recipe adjustment (-0.3% extract, +0.1% citric acid) signed off by R&D.
- **Sherbet crystals:** Fizzwick Confectionery. Crystals must arrive in sealed foil with desiccant. Reject any delivery with clumping.
- **Cultured butter:** Dalesfield Dairy, weekly delivery Monday.
- **Opaque liners:** PackRight, 3-week lead time. Reorder when stock falls below 40,000 liners.

Supplier price changes above 5% need Finance Director approval.
