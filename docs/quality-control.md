# Quality Control SOP: Blue Sherbet Line

1. Every batch (about 2,400 cookies) is sampled: 12 cookies from start, middle and end of the tray run.
2. Colourimeter check: L* 68-74 and b* -18 to -24. Outside range means quarantine the whole batch.
3. Fizz test: one cookie dissolved in 50ml warm water must show visible sherbet fizz within 20 seconds. No fizz means crystals degraded by humidity; check the crystal store hygrometer (must read under 45% RH).
4. Weight check: baked cookies 54g +/-2g.
5. Grey-green cookies: almost always crystals added too early or oven over 168C. Log under defect code CLR-02.
6. Quarantined batches are never reworked into new dough. They go to the staff shop or animal-feed partner after QA manager sign-off.
7. Allergens: contains wheat, milk, egg, soy. The line is nut-free but shares a site with the pistachio product (Green Moon). Full clean-down and ATP swab required at changeover.
