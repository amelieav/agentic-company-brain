# Blue Sherbet Cookie: Master Recipe and Colour Spec (v4.2)

Moonpool Bakehouse makes one hero product: the Blue Sherbet Cookie (SKU BSC-12, box of 12).

## Dough
- Base: 38% pastry flour, 22% cultured butter, 18% caster sugar, 9% pasteurised egg, 6% white chocolate chip, 4% citric-acid sherbet crystals, 3% vanilla and sea salt.
- Dough rests 14 hours at 3C before portioning. Resting under 12 hours causes spread and loses the domed top.
- Portion weight: 62g raw, 54g baked.

## Colour
- Colour comes from spirulina extract (phycocyanin), never synthetic dye. Target shade is Pantone 2905 C, "Moonpool Blue".
- Phycocyanin fades above 68C internal temperature and under pH 5.5. Bake at 165C for 11 minutes, max core temp 66C.
- Sherbet crystals are added AFTER mixing the dough, folded in for no more than 40 seconds, or they dissolve and the cookie turns grey-green.
- Batches failing a colourimeter reading of L* 68-74, b* -18 to -24 are quarantined (see quality-control.md).

## Shelf life
- 21 days sealed at ambient temperature below 22C. Light exposure bleaches the blue, so boxes use opaque inner liners.
