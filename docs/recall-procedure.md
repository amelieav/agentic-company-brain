# Recall and Incident Procedure

- **Level 1 (cosmetic):** colour or shape defect, no safety risk. QA manager decides; customers offered credit.
- **Level 2 (quality):** loss of shelf life, e.g. moisture ingress. Notify affected wholesalers within 24 hours.
- **Level 3 (safety):** allergen cross-contact (especially pistachio from the Green Moon line), foreign body, or microbiological failure. Stop shipments immediately, notify the Food Standards Agency within 24 hours, and the exec team is informed straight away.

Only an exec may declare a Level 3 recall. Trace by batch code; target to identify all affected customers within 4 hours.
