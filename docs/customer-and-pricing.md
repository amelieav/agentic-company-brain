# Customers, Pricing and Returns

- Wholesale price: 14.40 per box of 12 (BSC-12). Retail RRP: 22.00.
- Volume discount: 5% over 200 boxes/month, 8% over 500. Anything beyond 8% needs a manager.
- Seasonal edition "Frost Moon" (white-chocolate drizzle) runs November to January only, priced +10%.
- Payment terms: 30 days. Reminder at 14 days overdue, phone call at 30, credit hold at 60.
- Returns: boxes arriving with more than 3 broken cookies of 12 qualify for a full credit. Colour complaints (grey or green cookies) are treated as a quality incident and must be reported to QA the same day with batch code photos.
- Batch codes are printed on the box base as MB-YYWW-L (year, week, line).
